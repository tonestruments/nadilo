# Sounds

Made by `scripts/samples/cc0.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Every sound is a recording of a real instrument from a free sample library released under CC0, the public domain dedication of Creative Commons: anyone may use, change and share them, for any purpose, without asking and without credit. We name the libraries anyway, with thanks.

- Virtuosity Drums and Swirly Drums, by Karoryfer Samples
- Big Little Bass, by Karoryfer Samples
- Osiris Piano, by Karoryfer Samples
- [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) (VCSL), by Versilian Studios

Nothing sounds below 80 Hz, because laptop speakers crackle below that. Lowest is the lowest strong frequency in the file. Below 80 Hz is the share of the energy below 80 Hz. Loudness is measured above 150 Hz, as a laptop speaker plays it, and matches the sound each one replaced. A drum with four takes is played one take after another, never the same one twice running.
