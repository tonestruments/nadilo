# Nadilo School, level by level

The app keeps its words short on purpose. This guide has the longer story: what each level asks, what it is
quietly teaching, and the words for it. (Why School works this way: [GAME_DESIGN.md](GAME_DESIGN.md).)

## How a level works

School starts by playing you a short song, *First light*, whole. Then you rebuild it, one part at a time, one bar at a
time.

The song has its own player: Play, a track you can press or drag to move through it, and the song's parts under the
track (Intro, Verse, Break, Chorus, Outro). Press a part to hear it on its own, over and over; press it again and the
whole song plays on.

- **One bar on screen.** The song loops four bars. The strip above the instrument shows all four, small; press one
  to work on it. Each bar is its own: changing bar 2 never changes bar 3.
- **The song or Yours.** *The song* is the song, with the parts you have already rebuilt playing in it. *Yours* is
  the song with your notes in place of its own for this part. Switch between them while it plays: the music never
  stops, so you hear the difference at the same moment of the bar. When Yours sounds like The song, you have it.
  Press the one that is playing to stop it.
- **Up close**, for either: just the part you are working on and the one part it plays with. Up close on Yours is
  your own part with its partner.
- **Bar n or all four** (the loop says which bar it loops), and **Slow** (three quarters of the speed), for the hard
  bits.
- **Back** in the title bar goes one step back: the level before, or the song before the first level.
- **Marks** on the instrument show where the song's notes go, at first. Later only the first bar or beat is marked,
  then nothing: then it is by ear, and Hint marks the bar on screen.
- **A bar sews shut** (a tick in the strip) when it is exactly the song.
- **Stars**: one for meeting the goal, two for three quarters of the way to the song, three for exactly the song. You
  never need three to move on, and they never go down.
- Space plays and stops. ← and → move between bars. ⌘Z undoes.

## Pulse: the drums

The drums are drawn as steps: slots in the bar, each a moment where a hit can go.

| Level | You | It is called |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Heartbeat | Put a Boom on 1, 2, 3 and 4 in bar 1 | The **kick drum**. 1, 2, 3, 4 are the **beats**; four make a **bar** |
| Copy it on | Copy bar 1 into bars 2, 3 and 4 | The same bar four times is a **phrase**; played again and again, a **loop** |
| Clap back | Add a Crack on beats 2 and 4, in every bar | The **snare drum**, on the **backbeat** |
| Zoom in | A Tick on every number and every &, every bar | The **hi-hat**, on **eighth notes**: 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & |
| The turn | Find what is different about bar 4 | A **fill**: two quick snares lead back to bar 1. Each step is now a **sixteenth note** |

Each world ends on the whole song, two ways: *The song*, as you first heard it, and *Yours so far*, only what you have
made yet (after Pulse, your drums alone). Switch between them while it plays. The way on from a world's last level
says *Finish Pulse* (or the world's name).

## The Floor: the bass

The steps fall away. The bar is four beats, and a note starts on a beat and *lasts*: press a beat to place a note,
drag right to hold it, drag up or down to change how high it is. Six heights; a bigger dot is deeper, the ringed one
is home.

| Level | You | It is called |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under the boom | Trace bar 1's three notes; drag the first to hold it for two beats | The **bass line**. Two beats long is a **half note**, one beat a **quarter note** |
| Moods move | Bars 2 to 4: the same rhythm, new heights, by ear | Each bar's bass starts on its **root**, the note the bar is built on (in Color you meet the chord built on it) |

## Color: the chords

The beats fall away too. One colour fills the whole bar.

| Level | You | It is called |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paint the bars | Give each bar the colour you hear | **Chords**: three notes at once. One that lasts the whole bar is a **whole note** |

The song's four chords are C minor, A♭ major, E♭ major and B♭ major: i, VI, III, VII.

## Voice: the tune

| Level | You | It is called |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A tune | Trace bar 1's three notes | A **melody** |
| Echo | Make bar 3 sing bar 1 again (copy it) | A **motif**: a short idea that comes back |
| Answer it | Find bars 2 and 4 by ear, ending on the ringed height | **Call and response**, and **coming home** |

## Story: the whole song

The strip becomes the song's five sections: Intro, Verse, Break, Chorus, Outro. On each section's screen, switch each
part on or off.

| Level | You | It is called |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The whole song | Take parts out where the song does | The **arrangement** |

Then your song plays: every part rebuilt by you, with your changes in it.

## Make it yours

Pulse, Color and Voice each end with one more level. Your own song starts as the song, note for note; whatever you
change is **gold**. The song and Yours now compare exactly what you changed. When you like it, **Keep it**.

| Level | You | It is called |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your fill | Change bar 4's drums, your way. Try a take: Busier, Emptier, Surprise | A **variation** |
| Your colours | Give a bar a new colour; the bass moves with it | A new **mood** |
| Your answer | Answer bar 1 in bars 2 and 4, still ending on the ring | A **tune of your own** |

Undo takes back any change or take. *Back to the song's* puts the level's bars back.

## Your song

At the end of the journey (and from Home): the whole song, The song or Yours, and every part of every bar as a tile,
gold where it holds a change. While the song plays, the bar it is in is ringed in each part that plays there, and a
part that rests there fades. If nothing differs from the song yet, there is only the song to play. Press a tile to loop that bar; The song and Yours then swap just that part, so you hear
what your change did. Press it again to stop.

## Today's twist

Once something is yours, Home offers one small change a day to your song: one bar, one rule (keep the backbeat, end on
home). It is the same all day and new tomorrow. Keep it and it is sewn into your sampler, and into your song. Miss a day
and nothing is lost.

There are twenty twists, five or so for each part. Once every part is rebuilt there is a new one each day to the end of
October, and a twist you have already sewn is not offered again while another is left.
